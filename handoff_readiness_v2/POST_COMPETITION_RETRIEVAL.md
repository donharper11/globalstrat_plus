# Post-competition retrieval assessment

Team result APIs use explicit round numbers and do not require active/current
game state. Instructor Team Overview → View decisions offers historical rounds;
it now includes snapshot, lock actor/time and save-by-save audit evidence.

Existing data can produce an end-run summary of decisions, market conditions,
events, financial/product/market outcomes, performance/coherence/resilience,
rank, published rival aggregates, timestamps and actors. No new capture is
needed; building the report is out of scope.

Post-close browser captures for both roles have not yet been added, so V2-E is
pending even though the API/code paths support it.
