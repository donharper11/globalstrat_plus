# Recommended competition rules text

## Deadlines and submissions

All deadlines are determined by the platform server clock and published in China Standard Time and UTC. A submission is accepted only when the platform displays a receipt containing team, round, accepted timestamp and submission ID. Draft saves are not final submissions. The latest accepted final submission received before the deadline is used.

Late submissions are rejected uniformly. If no final submission is accepted by the deadline, the platform uses the team's latest saved draft as of the deadline; if no draft exists, the published default/no-action decision is used. The receipt and applied fallback are retained in the audit record.

## Disconnections and outages

A participant-side connection or device failure does not change the deadline. A platform-wide outage confirmed by the organiser pauses the competition for all teams. The organiser will publish a replacement deadline no shorter than the outage plus a 30-minute recovery period. Decisions will not be accepted through private messages, email or other side channels.

## Ties

Final ranking uses the published performance-index formula without discretionary adjustment. Exact ties are broken in this order: cumulative operating cash flow, cumulative revenue, then the better final-round resilience score. If still tied, the prize is shared equally; no random tie-break is used.

## Corrections

An organiser may correct a proven platform or data-entry error only through the logged correction function. The original submission remains visible in the audit record, and the correction records actor, reason, timestamp and before/after payload. Strategic regret is not grounds for correction.

## Disputes

A team must file a dispute within 24 hours of provisional results, identifying the round and submission/result receipt. The organiser will decide from the immutable submission record, resolution input manifest, stored seed, output manifest and system logs. If the recorded result cannot be reconstructed exactly, the affected round will be restored and replayed for all teams from the verified pre-resolution snapshot. The organiser will publish the ruling and any ranking change.
